The Philosophy

I don't just photograph weddings.
I document love.

We MegWedd — a documentary-style wedding photographer based in Kerala. I believe the best moments are the ones nobody planned: the way your grandmother wipes a tear, the nervous glance you share before walking in, the first breath of relief after you say "I do."

With a background in photojournalism, I work invisibly — present but never intrusive — so your memories feel lived, not posed. Every wedding is a unique story. My job is simply to listen, and let the light do the rest.
